I'm a software engineer at [Agoric](https://agoric.com/), and an open Web advocate and open source developer since 1993.

- **Building more cooperation with less vulnerability.** Since 2019 I've built [Agoric's smart contract platform](https://github.com/Agoric/agoric-sdk) with [Mark S. Miller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_S._Miller) and Dean Tribble, leaders in [object-capability security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Object-capability_model). Tribble led the first electronic check project and, at Microsoft, designed the Midori capability-based operating system. See [awesome-ocap](https://github.com/dckc/awesome-ocap) for ocap apps, libraries, and operating systems.
- **Helped grow W3C and its people.** [At W3C from 1995 to 2010](https://www.w3.org/blog/2010/thanks-for-a-great-15-years-at/), I co-edited HTML 2.0 with Tim Berners-Lee, recruited staff and members as the consortium grew from 13 organizations to over 500, and trained many[^aaron] in Web architecture and the art of consensus.
- **Built clinical research data infrastructure.** At the University of Kansas Medical Center from 2010 to 2021, I was technical lead for [HERON](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22195209/), a research data warehouse of 1.5 billion facts from hospital records, and then for software development across the [Greater Plains Collaborative](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24778202/), a ten-center clinical research network.
